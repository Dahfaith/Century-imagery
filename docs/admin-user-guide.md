# Admin User Guide — Century Imagery

Welcome to the Century Imagery Admin Dashboard. This platform gives you complete control over your cinematic portfolio, services, journal, and bookings.

## 1. Logging In and Out
- **Login:** Navigate to `https://centuryimagery.com/admin/login` and enter your credentials.
- **Logout:** In the admin dashboard, click your profile avatar in the navigation sidebar (or the mobile menu) and select **Log Out**.

## 2. Managing Portfolio Projects
- Go to the **Projects** tab.
- Click **Add Project** to create a new case study.
- Fill in the title, client, category, and descriptions.
- **Media:** Attach a cover image and a hero video. 
- You can mark projects as `published` when ready to go live, or keep them as `draft`.

## 3. Uploading Videos and Images
- Navigate to the **Media** tab.
- Click **Upload Media**. You can upload heavy videos directly to Cloudflare Stream and large imagery to Cloudflare R2.
- The system automatically transcodes your videos for optimal playback on mobile and desktop devices.
- You can browse the Media Library and attach these assets to pages, projects, or services.

## 4. Managing Services
- Go to the **Services** tab.
- Edit your core offerings (e.g., Commercial Videography, The Century Post Lab).
- Add high-quality cover videos to demonstrate the service capabilities.

## 5. Editing Website Pages
- Navigate to the **Pages** tab.
- You can edit the SEO titles, descriptions, and structural copy for public-facing pages (e.g., Home, About).
- Note: Core structural changes to the cinematic layout are handled by the engineering team, but the copy can be customized here.

## 6. Publishing Journal Articles
- Go to the **Journal** tab.
- Write deep dives into your production process, equipment choices, and behind-the-scenes stories.
- Assign a cover image and mark the article as `published` when you're ready to share it with your audience.

## 7. Reviewing and Managing Bookings
- Navigate to the **Bookings** tab.
- Client inquiries from the public website automatically appear here.
- You can review their budget, preferred dates, and message.
- Use the **Admin Notes** section to keep track of internal thoughts or follow-up statuses.

## 8. Updating Settings and SEO
- Go to the **Settings** tab.
- Update global contact information (Email, Phone, WhatsApp) and social media links.
- Modify the global SEO Title and SEO Description to optimize how Century Imagery appears on Google and social media preview cards.
